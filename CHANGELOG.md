# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — City streets and chunk LOD

- Reworked road hierarchy with arterial and local streets.
- Instanced road slabs, sidewalks, curbs, lane markings, crosswalks, trees and street lights.
- Added simple road signs and cycling traffic signals.
- Repositioned buildings into city blocks and reserved space for landmarks.
- Added active-chunk facade/window and rooftop detail LOD.
- Corrected chunk-to-world alignment and bounded the 6 km × 6 km play area.
- Added relative Vite base path for GitHub Pages project deployments.
- Improved touch pointer capture and reverse steering/camera follow direction.
